Kan Paarvai - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19 Oct 2026

About Kan Paarvai

Kan Paarvai is an educational accessibility application designed to help visually impaired students. It helps students understand images received on WhatsApp through voice descriptions (Tamil & English) and helps create documents using voice-over commands.

What Data We Collect

  • WhatsApp images sent by users for voice description
  • Voice commands for document creation
  • Phone number for sending back voice replies

How We Use Data

  • Images are processed ONLY to generate voice description (using AI) to help visually impaired students understand the image content.
  • We do NOT store images permanently. Images are deleted after processing within 24 hours.
  • We do NOT share your images or phone number with any third party.
  • We do NOT use data for advertising.

Data Storage

We temporarily process images on secure servers. No images are stored long-term. Voice descriptions are sent back via WhatsApp only.

Children & Students

Our app is designed to help students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental guidance. Students should use the app with teacher/parent support.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data anytime by messaging "DELETE MY DATA" on WhatsApp to our number.

Terms of Service

Kan Paarvai is an educational assistive tool. It provides AI-generated descriptions of images to help visually impaired students learn. Descriptions are for educational assistance only. The service is free for educational use.

Disclaimer: AI descriptions are for educational help and may not be 100% accurate. Users should verify important information with a teacher or trusted person.
